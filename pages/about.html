About CyberVault AI

Your Intelligent Cyber Defense Platform is built to make cybersecurity accessible, educational, and actionable for everyone.

Our Mission

CyberVault AI was created with a singular mission: to democratize cybersecurity education and make powerful security tools accessible to everyone, not just security professionals.

In a world where 2,200+ cyberattacks happen every day and cybercrime costs the global economy over $8 trillion annually, knowledge is the most powerful defense. CyberVault AI bridges the gap between complex cybersecurity concepts and everyday users.

Whether you're a student learning about ethical hacking, a business professional protecting sensitive data, or a home user wanting to stay safe online, CyberVault AI has the tools and knowledge to protect you.

About the Creator

The person behind CyberVault AI

Hello! I'm Vidhi Bhurani, an IBM Cybersecurity Bootcamp learner. CyberVault AI was created to help students, professionals, and everyday internet users improve their cybersecurity awareness through interactive tools, AI-powered guidance, threat analysis, learning modules, quizzes, and security assessments.

This project combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript, cybersecurity concepts, AI-powered assistance, and modern user experience design principles to create a practical cybersecurity learning platform.

My goal is to make cybersecurity education simple, engaging, and accessible for everyone.
